This is a project where the applicants save their details in the bank's data logger. The system asks you for your name and will give out the details which includes the Yearly income and credit score. Then, the system asks you about how much loan is required to you, and then it will also ask you how much deposit are you willing to pay. After that, the system starts processing the Loan to Income % and how much are times of your income are you taking a loan of, if it is more than 5 times your income, it is rejected, else it is approved. This is a ratio where the bank decides how much times of your income can be approved for loan, normally it is about 4.5 to 5x. Then comes the Loan to value ratio, which is the percentage of how much loan are you going to pay off on the deposit, if it is less than 5% of the loan, the loan is rejected. Lastly, the credit score, which has to be more then 650 to be fully approved, or it has a change of being rejected if it is in the range of 600 to 650, else it is fully rejected.
